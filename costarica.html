Global Connections

This interactive globe visualizes illicit weapon and drug flows that start, end, or transit through Costa Rica.

  • Explore globallyClick and drag to rotate the globe and view transnational routes across the world.
  • Inspect specific flowsHover over or click active flow lines to view detailed metadata about each recorded trajectory.
  • Filter dataUse the map legend to toggle specific flow types on or off.

Flows reported at the national level are mapped to the geographic center (centroid) of the respective country.

Types of Illicit Flows

This interactive map displays illicit arms and drug flows starting, ending, or transiting through Costa Rica between 2022 and 2025. To explore geographic overlaps with broader regional instability, the map incorporates conflict data from the Uppsala Conflict Data Program (UCDP) covering 2015 to 2025.

  • Inspect specific flowsHover over or click active flow lines to view detailed metadata about each recorded trajectory.
  • Filter dataUse the map legend to toggle specific flow types on or off.

Flows reported at the national level are mapped to the geographic center (centroid) of the respective country.

Seizures

This map displays selected interdiction points for drugs and weapons across Costa Rican trafficking routes between 2022 and 2025. By overlaying seizure locations with active illicit flow lines, this visualization highlights how interventions intersect with known trafficking routes and regional hotspots.

  • Inspect specific flowsHover over or click active flow lines to view detailed metadata about each recorded trajectory.
  • Filter dataUse the map legend to toggle seizure data on or off.

Flows reported at the national level are mapped to the geographic center (centroid) of the respective country.

Socioeconomic and Security Correlates of Illicit Flows

This map examines local vulnerability and security across Costa Rica at the canton level. It overlays the Multidimensional Poverty Index (IPM) and the Public Security Index (PUB) directly with illicit flows and seizure data.

  • Multidimensional Poverty Index (IPM)Measures poverty at the canton level in Costa Rica. Higher values indicate higher levels of multidimensional poverty.
  • Public Security Index (PUB)Measures the level of public security at the canton level in Costa Rica. Higher values indicate better public security conditions, while lower values indicate greater insecurity.
  • Swipe & CompareUse the center slider bar to sweep left or right across the map to observe how flow routes and seizure hotspots overlap with poverty and security indicators.
  • Filter DataUse the map slider to change IPM to PUB touch specific flow types to access data

Flows reported at the national level are mapped to the geographic center (centroid) of the respective country.
